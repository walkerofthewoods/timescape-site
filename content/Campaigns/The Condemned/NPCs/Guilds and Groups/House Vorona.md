**House Vorona** run the city’s navy, the largest military organization of any kind in [Orden](https://steelcompendium.io/v2/Browse/rule/world/orden/). Their engineers perfected the secret of blackpowder and guard it jealously. The Imperial Navy’s cannons protect trade across [Orden](https://steelcompendium.io/v2/Browse/rule/world/orden/), placing Capital at the center of international affairs. Vorona’s Far Mariners, aka the marines, are the closest thing Capital has to a city-wide law enforcement organization.
